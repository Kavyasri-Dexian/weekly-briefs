Madhya Pradesh Weekly Mandi Summary — July 19, 2026 to July 25, 2026

Total arrivals across Madhya Pradesh markets for the week were 376,722.75 tonnes, -19.4% against the previous week. Wheat was the most-traded commodity by arrival volume at 138,257.57 tonnes, 36.7% of state arrivals, traded across 250 markets. Other leading commodities by arrival volume were Onion (78,384.64), Soyabean (42,029.07), Green Gram(Moong)(Whole) (24,691.97), Bengal Gram(Gram)(Whole) (12,114.52).

The largest week-on-week price increases were Chili Red +51.79% to Rs 8,807.56 (from Rs 5,802.5); Amla(Nelli Kai) +47.88% to Rs 9,812.75 (from Rs 6,635.71); Asgand +31.36% to Rs 14,936.36 (from Rs 11,370.35), while the largest declines were Mousambi(Sweet Lime) -18.74% to Rs 3,642.11 (from Rs 4,482.14); Lemon -15.7% to Rs 2,896.15 (from Rs 3,435.55); Jowar(Sorghum) -15.29% to Rs 2,042.4 (from Rs 2,411.17). Wheat's average price this week was Rs 2,535.14, compared with last week Rs 2,489.64 (+1.83%), the same week last month Rs 2,423.67 (+4.6%), and the same week last year Rs 2,575.66 (-1.57%).

Of 442 registered market yards, 307 reported at least once this week: 95 markets reported on all 7 days, 163 reported on 5-6 days, and 135 filed no return. Vijaypur APMC reported the most days, 7 of 7.

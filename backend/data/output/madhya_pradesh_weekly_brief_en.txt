Madhya Pradesh Weekly Mandi Summary — 2026-07-17 to 2026-07-23

Total arrivals across Madhya Pradesh markets for the week were 410,715.69 tonnes, a change of -8.3% over the previous week.
Wheat was the most-traded commodity by arrival volume (157,719.74 tonnes, 38.4% of state arrivals), traded across 252 markets.
Other leading commodities by arrival volume: Onion (77,621.8), Soyabean (50,698.21), Green Gram(Moong)(Whole) (23,961.9), Bengal Gram(Gram)(Whole) (13,727.53).
Largest week-on-week price increases: Water Melon +59.39% (Rs 1,733.33 from Rs 1,087.5); Bael +52.72% (Rs 6,300.33 from Rs 4,125.5); Asgand +36.53% (Rs 13,589.53 from Rs 9,953.31).
Largest week-on-week price decreases: Ridgeguard(Tori) -24.78% (Rs 1,255.0 from Rs 1,668.42); Ashwagandha -22.38% (Rs 15,288.89 from Rs 19,695.83); Soanf -20.42% (Rs 9,688.9 from Rs 12,175.0).
Of 442 registered market yards, 307 reported at least once this week. 111 markets reported on all 7 days, 155 reported on 5-6 days, and 135 filed no return.
Vijaypur APMC reported the most days (7 of the week).

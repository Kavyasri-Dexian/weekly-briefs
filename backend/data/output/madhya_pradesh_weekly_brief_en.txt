Madhya Pradesh Weekly Mandi Summary — July 14, 2026 to July 20, 2026

Total arrivals across Madhya Pradesh markets for the week were 457,686.36 tonnes, +0.3% against the previous week. Wheat was the most-traded commodity by arrival volume at 189,380.53 tonnes, 41.4% of state arrivals, traded across 248 markets. Other leading commodities by arrival volume were Onion (69,998.66), Soyabean (52,844.07), Green Gram(Moong)(Whole) (22,936.1), Bengal Gram(Gram)(Whole) (17,683.65).

The largest week-on-week price increases were Mango(Raw-Ripe) +52.62% to Rs 5,523.08 (from Rs 3,618.75); Bael +32.38% to Rs 5,070.4 (from Rs 3,830.2); Asgand +30.88% to Rs 12,058.53 (from Rs 9,213.21), while the largest declines were Chili Red -41.47% to Rs 6,430.7 (from Rs 10,987.6); Ashwagandha -26.92% to Rs 15,533.33 (from Rs 21,256.67); Mango Powder -18.33% to Rs 5,112.75 (from Rs 6,260.56). Wheat's average price this week was Rs 2,497.6, compared with last week Rs 2,471.16 (+1.07%), the same week last month Rs 2,408.06 (+3.72%), and the same week last year Rs 2,549.86 (-2.05%).

Of 442 registered market yards, 303 reported at least once this week: 111 markets reported on all 7 days, 153 reported on 5-6 days, and 139 filed no return. Vijaypur APMC reported the most days, 7 of 7.

Madhya Pradesh Weekly Mandi Summary — July 14, 2026 to July 20, 2026

Total arrivals across Madhya Pradesh markets for the week were 457,686.24 tonnes, a change of +0.3% over the previous week.
Wheat was the most-traded commodity by arrival volume (189,380.53 tonnes, 41.4% of state arrivals), traded across 248 markets.
Other leading commodities by arrival volume: Onion (69,998.66), Soyabean (52,844.07), Green Gram(Moong)(Whole) (22,936.1), Bengal Gram(Gram)(Whole) (17,683.65).
Largest week-on-week price increases: Mango(Raw-Ripe) +52.62% (Rs 5,523.08 from Rs 3,618.75); Bael +32.38% (Rs 5,070.4 from Rs 3,830.2); Asgand +30.88% (Rs 12,058.53 from Rs 9,213.21).
Largest week-on-week price decreases: Chili Red -41.47% (Rs 6,430.7 from Rs 10,987.6); Ashwagandha -26.92% (Rs 15,533.33 from Rs 21,256.67); mango powder -18.33% (Rs 5,112.75 from Rs 6,260.56).
Wheat's average price this week was Rs 2,497.6 — vs last week Rs 2,471.16 (+1.07%), same week last month Rs 2,408.06 (+3.72%), same week last year Rs 2,549.86 (-2.05%).
Of 442 registered market yards, 303 reported at least once this week. 111 markets reported on all 7 days, 153 reported on 5-6 days, and 139 filed no return.
Vijaypur APMC reported the most days (7 of 7 days).

Executive narrativemachine-generated from verified data · approved 20-Jul-2026 11:40 IST

Total arrivals across Madhya Pradesh during Week 29 were 31,42,800 quintals, an increase of 5.3 per cent over the previous week but 5 per cent below the three-year average for the same calendar week. Wheat accounted for 26.8 per cent of state arrivals at 8,42,600 quintals, followed by soybean at 19.0 per cent. Commodity concentration remained low at an HHI of 0.140.

The arrival-weighted modal price index declined 1.9 per cent. Garlic recorded the largest increase at 24.8 per cent to Rs 9,180 per quintal, while tomato recorded the largest decline at 31.4 per cent to Rs 860 per quintal alongside a 38.6 per cent rise in arrivals, raising the distress composite flag across 14 districts. Soybean traded at Rs 4,462 per quintal against a Minimum Support Price of Rs 5,528, a realisation of 80.7 per cent, with 251 of 286 trading market yards below the support price for a third consecutive week.

Of 543 active market yards, 498 reported on at least one day, giving a state compliance score of 87.4 per cent, an improvement of 2.8 percentage points. Forty-five market yards filed no return for the full week. Shahdol Division recorded the lowest divisional compliance at 71.6 per cent. Six Critical alerts are open. Data completeness for the week was 91.7 per cent.
